# Hybrid Stability — Android host and iPhone

Based on the Exynos Live AP bridge b011c75; no native PCM, modem, mixer or call-audio implementation changes. The Android Connection transport card selects Automatic, L2CAP only, or GATT only. Changes take effect on a fresh connection; disconnect/reconnect both apps outside a call. Automatic prefers GATT for five minutes after three L2CAP sessions shorter than 30 s within successive 120 s failure windows, per observed Bluetooth address. This is in-memory and not a guarantee of link-layer recovery. GATT-only masks the PSM and rejects incoming L2CAP; L2CAP-only refuses GATT data subscription (wake characteristic remains). Existing iPhone can use the Android transport selector.

The BLE stability profile defaults on for j7velte. Requests high connection priority once per keeper/priority change, keeps that priority instead of toggling at call boundaries, prefers LE 1M PHY, avoids requesting unsupported 2M PHY, logs actual negotiated interval, latency, supervision timeout and GATT disconnect status. The OS/controller/central ultimately negotiate parameters. No claim of setting 5000 ms, no root HCI commands or firmware modifications. Short session tracking may be reset by address rotation.

Optional Wi-Fi audio + BLE wake is disabled by default; enable on Android and install both updated apps. Join the same Wi-Fi or hotspot (private IPv4). Permit local network on iOS. Authenticated BLE control offers a private Wi-Fi listener; fresh mutual HMAC challenge and HKDF derive independent AES-GCM keys and counters. Only voice frames travel over the Wi-Fi lane; pairing, call control, SMS, sync and wake remain on BLE. Bounded queues and failed socket fallback keep old peers functional. iOS retries probe while BLE is up. Wi-Fi is subordinate to the BLE session and closes when BLE ends: it reduces BLE traffic, does not guarantee survival of complete BLE disconnect. Network isolation, permission denial or absence of usable interface means BLE remains active. No public listener, cloud, or account.

Test: enable Magisk Root UID 0 and Exynos AP bridge on j7velte, use consenting short calls. Compare idle and active calls in L2CAP-only and GATT-only, then Auto with Wi-Fi audio. Log actual transport, BLE interval/timeout, RTT, jitter, and Wi-Fi authentication. Turn Wi-Fi off mid-call and check BLE fallback; lock iPhone and check CallKit wake; verify SMS and ordinary handset call. End call immediately if sound abnormal. Hardware validation remains necessary. IPA is unsigned; requires user signing (e.g. AltStore/SideStore where supported).
